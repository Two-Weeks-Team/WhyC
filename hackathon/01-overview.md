**Source:** https://rapid-agent.devpost.com/

## Title & Theme

**Google Cloud Rapid Agent Hackathon: Building Agents for Real-World Challenges**

Tagline: *"AI that doesn't just provide answers — it helps you take action."*
Focus: agents that **accomplish tasks** (not chatbots).

## Prize Structure

**Total Pool: $30,000** across three partner tracks.

Per partner track (Arize / Elastic / Fivetran):

| Place      | Prize        |
| ---------- | ------------ |
| 1st        | $5,000 USD + social-media promotion |
| 2nd        | $3,000 USD   |
| 3rd        | $2,000 USD   |

A submission can win **a maximum of one prize**.

## Eligibility (high-level)

- Above legal majority age in your jurisdiction (21+ in Taiwan)
- **Excluded jurisdictions**: Italy, Brazil, Quebec, Crimea, Cuba, Iran, Syria, North Korea, Sudan, Belarus, Russia, Afghanistan, Antarctica, China, Djibouti, Iraq, Kazakhstan, Somalia, Venezuela, Vietnam, Western Sahara
- Not on OFAC SDN or Department of Commerce Denied/Unverified lists
- **Disqualifying affiliations**: Employees, interns, contractors, official office-holders of Google, Partner Entities, parent/subsidiary/affiliate companies are ineligible

---

## What Participants Build

> "Build a functional agent — powered by **Gemini** and **Google Cloud Agent Builder** — that integrates a **Partner Entity's MCP server** to solve a real challenge."

Beyond Q&A: must execute multi-step tasks. Solve real-world problems in work, personal life, or hobbies.

---

## Submission Components (required)

1. **Public open-source code repository** (with OSI-approved license file in About section)
2. **Hosted project URL** (functional, accessible to judges)
3. **Demo video ≤ 3 minutes** (YouTube or Vimeo, English or subtitled)
4. **Text description** (features, technologies, data sources, learnings)
5. **Track selection** (Arize, Elastic, or Fivetran — choose one)
6. **Completed Devpost submission form**

---

## Judging Criteria (Stage Two, equal weight)

1. **Technological Implementation** — does interaction with Google Cloud and Partner services demonstrate quality software development?
2. **Design** — is UX/design well thought out?
3. **Potential Impact** — how big an impact on target communities?
4. **Quality of the Idea** — how creative and unique?

Stage One is pass/fail (all components present, addresses challenge, applies Partner data + Google Cloud effectively).

Tie-breaker: criterion scores in listed order; if still tied, judges vote.
